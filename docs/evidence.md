## Evidence policy

Every substantive Korean claim should resolve to data/korean/sources.csv and, where appropriate, data/korean/evidence.csv. A source citation is not treated as blanket evidence for every derived field.

Competing analyses are retained in data/korean/analyses.csv.

## Current evidence boundary

Normative rule coverage is strong for the implemented rule classes. Corpus-scale lexical attestation, exhaustive morphology and exhaustive dialect/age variation are not yet represented. Absence from the registry is therefore not evidence of impossibility.
