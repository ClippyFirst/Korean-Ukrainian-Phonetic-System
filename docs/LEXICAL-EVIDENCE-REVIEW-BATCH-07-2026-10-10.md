# Lexical evidence review — batch 07 (2026-10-10)

## Scope

This batch reviews ten queue records. Nine have direct NIKL evidence for the exact surface form or are explicit examples in the official rule text; their rule notes have now been normalized to cite the relevant numbered articles. One record, `넓어졌다는`, remains a compositional example backed only by the generic rules page and is deliberately left in the unresolved queue.
