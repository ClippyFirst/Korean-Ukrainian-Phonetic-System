## Scope

The generic official rules page is authoritative for the rule text, but it is not automatically direct evidence for every word or phrase listed in the project.

## Findings and policy

1. A general *표준 발음법* page can support a derivation, but the data should not imply that it explicitly contains a word or phrase when it does not.
2. Inflected forms assembled from multiple sources should preserve the provenance of each component. `찍었어요` is the clearest case in this batch: the note describes two pieces of evidence but the schema currently stores one URL.
3. Phrase/corpus records such as `국립국어원에서` and `나중엔` need provenance labels that differ from exact dictionary or official rule examples.
4. Do not assign article numbers from a process label alone. Where mappings above are marked “candidate”, they remain proposed rule explanations, not a completed source-to-article audit.
5. Ukrainian target quality remains a separate review dimension; this batch does not validate the Ukrainian syllable strings or the broad IPA.
