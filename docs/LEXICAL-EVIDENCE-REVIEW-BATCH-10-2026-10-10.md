# Lexical evidence review — batch 10 (2026-10-10)

## Scope

This batch reviews ten records at the head of the remaining traceability queue. Nine are source-backed pronunciation examples and now have explicit rule mappings. `대한민국` is intentionally treated differently: it is a baseline control with no Korean surface rewrite, so this audit does not invent an article mapping merely to clear it from the queue.

## Batch result

- Nine records now have explicit article mappings.
- The unresolved traceability queue decreases from **48 to 39**.
- The contrast between 밭이 (§17) and 밭을 (§13) is explicit and tested.
- The audit preserves a baseline control rather than forcing every unchanged proper name into a numbered-rule explanation.
- No Korean surface output, Ukrainian target, or IPA value was changed in this batch.
