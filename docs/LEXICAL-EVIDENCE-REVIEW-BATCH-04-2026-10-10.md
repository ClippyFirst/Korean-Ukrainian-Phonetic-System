## Scope

## Findings

- A common generic rules URL is not automatically a bad URL, but it is **not proof that the page explicitly contains the whole word**. The record's evidence type must make that distinction visible.
- Rows with no Korean surface change (`머리를`, `예쁘고`, `보러`, `일찍`) should not be forced into a numbered article mapping just because they occur in a traceability queue.
- Candidate article mappings in this batch are hypotheses for the audit trail, not final normative assignments. The exact ordered derivation should be verified before using them as implementation requirements.
- The Ukrainian targets and IPA transcriptions have not been validated by this source review.
