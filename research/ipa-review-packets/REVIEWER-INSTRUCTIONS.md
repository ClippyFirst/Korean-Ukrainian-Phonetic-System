# Independent pronunciation review — reviewer instructions

You are receiving one pilot packet containing Korean input forms and source-row identifiers. Please work independently and do not seek or infer the identity of any software system, comparison baseline, or previous transcription.

## Task

1. Restore the complete source utterance for each listed source-row identifier before scoring. If the full context is unavailable, mark the item `NEEDS_CONTEXT`; do not guess from the isolated token.
2. Provide a broad phonemic IPA transcription for each item.
3. Optionally provide a separate narrow phonetic transcription, identifying exactly which predictable allophonic details you represent.
4. Record syllable boundaries and vowel length only under a stated convention and supported evidence.
5. Classify each disputed detail as phonemic, predictable allophony, transcription convention, lexical variation, or unresolved.
6. Cite primary sources or describe the phonetic evidence, method, and confidence.
7. Do not infer a general rule from this small pilot sample.

## Submission

For every item, provide broad IPA, optional narrow IPA, syllable boundaries, vowel-length assessment, analysis category, source/evidence, confidence, and notes or unresolved questions.

Please submit your first-pass decisions independently. Do not discuss or reconcile differences with another reviewer until both first-pass submissions have been frozen.

## Readiness

These are pilot manifests, not complete scoring packets. Full source utterance context and per-item reviewer-sharing permission must be verified before scoring begins. If either is unavailable, leave the item unresolved and state the blocker.
